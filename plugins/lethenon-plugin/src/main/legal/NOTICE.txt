The Lethenon plugin of mystic-crypt-ui, and what its zip carries besides its own classes
=====================================================================================

The plugin's own classes are under the MIT License of mystic-crypt-ui. Its zip also carries two
libraries under lib/, which are not covered by that licence. Their licence texts are in this
directory, taken unchanged from the jars they belong to.

lib/lethenon-<version>.jar
    lethenon, the chain library: https://github.com/astrapi69/lethenon
    MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 Asterios Raptis
    The licence: lethenon/LICENSE.txt

lib/sqlite-jdbc-<version>.jar
    SQLite JDBC by Xerial: https://github.com/xerial/sqlite-jdbc
    Apache License, Version 2.0. The licence: sqlite-jdbc/LICENSE
    It contains code of the Zentus SQLiteJDBC driver, Copyright (c) 2006, David Crawshaw, under
    the licence in sqlite-jdbc/LICENSE.zentus, which asks for its notice in every binary
    distribution - this is that notice.
    It carries a native build of SQLite for each platform it supports. SQLite is in the public
    domain: https://sqlite.org/copyright.html
