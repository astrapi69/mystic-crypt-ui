The Wi-Fi QR plugin of mystic-crypt-ui, and what its zip carries besides its own classes
=====================================================================================

The plugin's own classes are under the MIT License of mystic-crypt-ui. Its zip also carries one
library under lib/, which is not covered by that licence. Its licence text is in this directory,
taken from the library's sources, since its jar carries none.

lib/qrcodegen-<version>.jar
    QR Code generator library by Project Nayuki: https://www.nayuki.io/page/qr-code-generator-library
    MIT License, Copyright (c) Project Nayuki. The licence: qrcodegen/LICENSE.txt, the header of the
    library's own sources, unchanged (qrcodegen-1.8.0-sources.jar, io/nayuki/qrcodegen/QrCode.java).
