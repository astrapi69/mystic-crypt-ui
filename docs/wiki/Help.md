# Help

Where to start, depending on what you want to do.

- **First time here:** [[Getting-Started]] walks through the first launch and your first database.
- **A new database:** [[create-new-mystic-crypt-database]] explains the dialog that creates one.
- **Your database:** [[open-existing-mystic-crypt-database]] explains the dialog that opens it, and
  [[Signing-In]] what master password, key file and locking the workspace mean.
- **Coming from KeePass:** [[import-export-keepass-database]].
- **A password nobody should read over your shoulder:** [[Secure-your-passwords-with-obfuscation]].
- **A plugin from somewhere else:** [[install-a-plugin]].
- **A wallet for LETH:** [[Plugin-Lethenon-Wallet]], with the backup of its 24 words.
- **Your OpenPGP release key:** [[Plugin-Release-Key]] shows what your GnuPG home holds.

For everything else, the pages under "The application" and "The tools" in the sidebar describe each
part of the program, and [[Home]] says what the application is for.
